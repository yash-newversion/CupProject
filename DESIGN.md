## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an experience of grounded luxury and sustainable indulgence. It balances the warmth of culinary craft with the precision of contemporary direct-to-consumer e-commerce.

- **Brand Personality**: Artisanal, tactile, responsible, refined, and sensory-rich.
- **Target Audience**: Discerning consumers, specialty coffee purveyors, boutique hospitality buyers, and sustainability-conscious epicureans seeking waste-free culinary elegance.
- **Emotional Response**: A feeling of quiet indulgence, earthy warmth, effortless craftsmanship, and mindful delight.
- **Design Movement**: **Tactile Organic Modernism** — bridging quiet luxury with organic warmth. The aesthetic avoids cold clinical e-commerce tropes in favor of deep natural tones, refined editorial typography, generous whitespace, pill-and-soft-corner geometries, and diffuse ambient shadows that echo natural ambient light falling across textured surfaces.

## Colors

The palette is derived from sun-cured olive groves, baked wheat crumb, and unbleached botanical linen. 

- **Primary (`#596B35`)**: The core brand signature. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key interactive indicators, and primary navigation focus.
- **Secondary / Deep Olive (`#39452B`)**: Dense botanical base for high-contrast typography, dark-surface product anchors, footers, and key editorial headline treatments.
- **Tertiary / Muted Golden Accent (`#D5A65A`)**: Evokes baked crusts, caramel notes, and honey glaze. Reserved strictly for conversion accents, badges, artisanal highlights, and rating stars.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#E9E5D2`)**: Light Sage Cream serving as the primary backdrop foundation, establishing a warm, tactile, non-sterile atmosphere.
- **Supporting Accents**:
  - **Soft Green (`#EDF0E5`)**: Container fills, tertiary interactive chip surfaces, and muted backgrounds for product display cards.
  - **Charcoal (`#292D24`)**: Deepest body copy, icon glyphs, and structural lines where pure black would create harsh unnatural friction.
  - **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Crisp foreground cards, raised modals, and interactive inputs to lift focus off the cream background.

## Typography

The type system pairs the editorial elegance of **Playfair Display** with the clear geometric warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts**: Playfair Display provides literary sophistication and culinary authority. It commands product narrative titles, ingredient highlights, and hero showcases.
- **UI, Product Specs & Continuous Text**: Plus Jakarta Sans delivers pristine legibility across catalog grids, nutritional charts, ordering controls, and transaction flows.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Rendered in uppercase or bold weights of Plus Jakarta Sans with generous tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to impart a curated, boutique-label appearance on flavor tags, certifications, and volume badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy relies on an intentional, generous fluid grid built for expansive visual breathing room.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout with a maximum container width of `1320px`, `1.5rem` gutters, and dynamic margins of at least `3rem`. Elements span asymmetric blocks (e.g., 5-column narrative copy balancing a 7-column product stage) to evoke culinary magazine layouts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. Multi-tiered recipe and bundle cards collapse cleanly from 4 to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Complex purchasing modules stack into vertical tactile cards with sticky checkout anchors.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: All margins and element distances adhere strictly to a warm 8-point rhythmic scale, reserving `space-xl` and above for editorial pacing between thematic product chapters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered tonal surfaces and ultra-diffused, warm-tinted ambient shadows that mimic natural daylight filtered through foliage.

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - **Floor / Base**: Light Sage Cream (`#E9E5D2`) creates the foundation.
  - **Surface Container**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Soft Green (`#EDF0E5`) for cards and inset trays.
  - **Overlay / High Focus**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for drawers, cart sheets, and dietary filters.
- **Shadow Philosophy**:
  - Shadows avoid neutral grayscale. They are tinted with Charcoal (`#292D24`) at minimal opacities (between 4% and 10%).
  - **Resting Elevation**: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(41, 45, 36, 0.05)`.
  - **Raised / Hover Elevation**: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(41, 45, 36, 0.08)`.
  - **Floating (Sticky Bars, Sheets)**: `0px 20px 48px -6px rgba(41, 45, 36, 0.12)`.
- **Ghost Borders**: To preserve organic softness without heavy outlines, cards and surface boundaries utilize a 1px border colored with `#39452B` at 8% opacity.

## Shapes

The design system embraces soft, rounded curves that reflect the artisanal craftsmanship and rounded geometries of cookie cups.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem`)**: Applied to compact controls, quantity adjusters, and form inputs.
- **Card & Container Radius (`1rem` to `1.5rem`)**: Product showcase cards, subscription selectors, and cart summary drawers feature generous `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl`) curvatures to maintain tactile warmth.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`)**: Fully rounded geometries are reserved for buttons, status chips, dietary tags (e.g., "Gluten-Free", "Fair-Trade Cocoa"), and circular imagery frames.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - **Primary**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), Deep Olive background (`#39452B`), White text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.875rem` uppercase label tracking. Hover state transitions softly to Primary Olive (`#596B35`) with subtle lift.
  - **Secondary / Accent**: Pill-shaped, Muted Gold background (`#D5A65A`), Deep Charcoal text (`#292D24`). Reserved for "Order Now" or limited-batch releases.
  - **Tertiary / Ghost**: Soft Green background (`#EDF0E5`), Primary Olive text (`#596B35`), 1px translucent border (`rgba(57, 69, 43, 0.12)`).
- **Cards**:
  - Primary product cards use Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with `1.5rem` corner radius (`rounded-2xl`), resting on the Light Sage Cream canvas. Cards feature 1px subtle ghost borders and diffuse warm drop-shadows. Image containers within cards carry a Soft Green (`#EDF0E5`) backing to complement natural product photography.
- **Chips & Tags**:
  - Micro-tags use `rounded-full` styling, padding of `0.25rem 0.75rem`, set in `label-sm`. Fills use Soft Green (`#EDF0E5`) with Primary Olive text (`#596B35`), or Muted Gold (`#D5A65A`) tinting for award-winning attributes.
- **Input Fields & Quantity Steppers**:
  - Rounded to `0.5rem` or `rounded-full` for steppers. Surfaces are Pure White with a 1px border of `#39452B` (15% opacity). On focus, borders transition smoothly to Primary Olive (`#596B35`) with an ambient 3px olive glow ring at 15% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Rounded check squares (`0.25rem`) and circular radio discs with a Deep Olive stroke. When checked, the fill transitions to Primary Olive (`#596B35`) with a crisp White marker.
- **Product Spec & Flavor Profiles**:
  - Dedicated tactile bars displaying tasting notes (e.g., "Crisp Vanilla", "Roasted Oat"), holding capacity (e.g., "60ml Espresso"), and heat resistance duration, styled with segmented pill containers over soft neutral backdrops.
